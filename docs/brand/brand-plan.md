# 3tched brand and positioning plan

Source: Emergent brand engagement 9955e772-bbb4-4fc6-81d1-a0580ad973fa. This is a brand direction and design brief, not implemented site code or a production vector identity. Refine the existing site, do not rebuild it. Retain the 3tched name, the "Etched in schema" tagline, and the digest-node motif.

## Positioning

**Control and accountability, as one operation.**

A single typed schema governs identity, authority, policy, and evidence, so proof is a byproduct of operating correctly, not a report assembled later. The brand must make three things credible: real infrastructure rather than an AI wrapper; accountability produced by operating rather than collected afterward; and one coherent, defended source of truth.

## Why now

Lead with agents, not regulation. AI agents act on live systems at machine speed, but after-the-fact audit cannot keep pace. Governance must move into the operating path, where identity, authority, provenance, and evidence remain attached to action. The movement from periodic audits toward continuous, provable control is a supporting accelerant.

## Audiences

- Investors: crisp positioning, defensible architecture, agent-first urgency.
- Enterprise security and GRC leaders: serious, restrained accountability without another bolted-on audit system.
- Platform engineers: concrete primitives and accurate technical claims.

## Messaging guardrails

- Say "tamper-evident / cryptographically verifiable state history" rather than leading with "hash-linked history."
- Say "AI and people act under the same identity, authority, and policy" rather than positioning AI as a gimmick.
- Not a blockchain or crypto project, not an AI wrapper on others' tools, and not after-the-fact compliance tooling.
- Keep founder story secondary: one coherent model, a defended single source of truth, and first-principles thinking.

## Visual direction

- Wordmark: "3tched", with the 3 tuned to carry the "etched" reading. Retain the monospace "Etched in schema" tagline. Clear-space and minimum-size rules remain to be finalized.
- Base colors: charcoal `#111111`, grey `#6B7280`, off-white `#FAFAF8`. One functional amber/ochre accent, starting at `#C6892F`, reserved for verification/evidence states. No green, default SaaS blue, decorative amber fills, or crypto sheen.
- Typography: neutral geometric sans for prose; precise monospace for schema, evidence, and technical labels.
- Motif: reusable digest-node / linked-record diagram language for schema, state, and evidence. Subtle motion only when clarifying state change or verification.
