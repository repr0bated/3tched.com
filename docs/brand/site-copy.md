## Hero

**Control and accountability, as one operation.**

3tched is a schema-driven operating environment where identity, authority, policy, state, and evidence stay connected, so proof is produced as the system operates.

Visual direction: schema branching into layers, with one verification node in amber. CTA: **Start a conversation.**

## The problem

**Most systems separate action from accountability.** Operational systems do the work, security tools control access, compliance collects evidence afterward, and AI reasons from summaries that may have drifted from live state. Every separation risks lost context, bypassed controls, and incomplete proof.

## One schema, every layer

A shared typed schema defines objects, available actions, required authority, and the evidence created when state changes. It informs live operational state, identity and authorization, policy enforcement, interfaces and tools, verifiable evidence, and governed access for people and AI. No separate compliance model has to be kept in sync with the real system.

## How it works

1. **Govern at the moment of action.** Evaluate who or what is acting, their authority, the attempted action, and the applicable policy.
2. **Keep state and evidence connected.** Accepted changes contribute to a tamper-evident, cryptographically verifiable history of what happened, who acted, and how the system reached its current state.
3. **Semantic retrieval, governed inference.** Reason over live governed context while identity, authority, and provenance remain attached, so the system can show which context was used, under what authority, backed by what evidence.

## Why it matters

**Control and accountability should be the same operation.** Traditional audit reconstructs events after the fact. 3tched is designed so correct operation produces supporting evidence, moving governance from periodic review to continuous operation.

## Why now

AI agents act on live systems faster than anyone can audit them after the fact. Accountability has to happen at the moment of action. 3tched is designed to make that structural. Regulatory demand for continuous, provable control increases the stakes, but is not the lead story.
